Return ranked content hypotheses: format, hook pattern, 3+ example posts with reach numbers and
links, why it travels, how it maps to the app without forcing the product into the story.

Scraping etiquette: never bulk-fetch through the owner's logged-in browser (ban risk) — a few pages
with pauses; download media with logged-out tools. Distinguish measured numbers from estimates.
