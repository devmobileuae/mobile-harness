Write the scenario as a shot table: t, shot, on-screen text, VO line, sound. Rules: hook lands in
the first 1.5 s with clarity + curiosity; tension over explanation; no product, score or brand in the
story unless the brief asks; every VO line names things unambiguously against its frame; ending is
unexpected, not a happy montage. Keep text inside safe zones (200 < y < 1500, x < 960).
