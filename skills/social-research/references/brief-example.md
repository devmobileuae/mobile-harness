# Research brief — the accepted shape

Files in `<app repo>/growth/research/`: `building-blocks.md`, `videos.json`, `hypotheses/1–4`,
`<platform>-teardown-<date>/teardown.md`. Written for the owner; numbers verbatim. Your real briefs: `local/skills/social-research/`.

## 1. Collection log (always state what was NOT done)

- TikTok: public pages of 12 hashtags → ~1,500 unique videos; ~490 in last 45 days; ~190 > 100K; ~30 > 1M.
- Instagram: public `/popular/<tag>/` pages; post date decoded from the shortcode.
- Stability: medians of 7 accounts over their last 17–93 videos.
- Comments read under 9 key videos.
- **Not done:** YouTube Shorts; full video teardown — vidIQ (0 credits, resets <date>), ScrapeCreators (402),
  AppKittie (402) were dry, so hooks were read from covers/first frames. Tag pages return "popular", not the
  full feed → top of the distribution. TikTok anti-bot "Please wait" page after ~40 loads → stopped.

## 2. Headline findings (3–5, each with numbers)

1. <Which kind of content spreads on which platform, with medians and ranges.>
2. <Which accounts in the niche grow this way: median per account; a counter-example with a weak hook.>
3. <Strongest comment template, verbatim, with likes.>
4. <A curiosity or identity driver, with the series numbers and follower count.>
5. <What else drives shares (pride, nostalgia…), with the top numbers.>

## 3. Pattern table

A pattern counts only if it appears at ≥ 2 authors or ≥ 3 videos of one author. Columns: format | examples (views) |
stability (median / n of n) | why it works.
