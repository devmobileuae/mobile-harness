## Format-level signals

- "Same race / same X, different player": a ~1K-follower app account at a ~180K median, going from zero to 7 of
  16 posts ≥ 250K in three weeks. Engagement is not the engine (a 791K post with 218 shares, 16 comments —
  pure watch-through). Wave-wide: 41 posts ≥ 1M among ~300, but a ~5.8K format median → plan several variants.
- Split-screen physical metaphors (procrastinator vs planner, two buckets, a brick a day): 0.6–8M on 1–34K
  accounts. Shared traits: a physical payoff (tower, full bucket), one repeated action with a click per repeat,
  6–15 s, 1–2-word labels, a counter, the moral as one closing line. A *number* as payoff doesn't land with outsiders.
- Small accounts (≤ 10K): skit + app demo (a paper strip "7:00 AM → 10:00 PM", then the app screen) reaching
  0.8–2.3M on 300–2K followers; stickman archetype podiums with several hits in a row; a screen recording of
  getting past a Screen Time limit. App accounts blow up when the product IS the punchline of a real-life skit.
- Quiz/game niches (e.g. geography): web-game and quiz-app accounts posting one format a day hold 30–50K
  medians; a bare «Guess the country, Level: Easy» app account sits at a few hundred — the hook mechanic decides,
  not the topic. «Very Easy» levels are rage bait (thousands of comments). On IG, quizzes stay modest (40–130K)
  while animated map explainers reach 2–40M.
